Fit Wallpaper: Support

Turn your favorite photos into wallpapers that fit your iPhone or iPad.

Contact Us

Open Settings > Contact Us in the app to send a question or feedback. Attaching images is optional. If you would like a reply, enter your email address.

You can also email us at contact@nexus-inc.net.

How do I set a saved image as my wallpaper?

After you tap Save in the app, open Settings > Wallpaper on your iPhone or iPad and choose the image from your photo library. Check the photo's position and size in the system preview.

Can I open a photo from the Photos app?

Yes. In the Photos app or another app, tap the Share button and choose Fit Wallpaper. Open Fit Wallpaper within 10 minutes to load the photo into the editor.

I can't find my device

Tap Select Device and search by model name. If your device isn't listed, let us know from Contact Us.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads

Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you used for the purchase, then tap Settings > Restore Purchases in the app.

Does Buy me a coffee remove ads?

No. It is an optional purchase to support the app's development. It is separate from Remove Ads and does not unlock any features.

How do I request a refund?

Use Apple's Report a Problem website. For details, see Apple's guide to requesting a refund. Nothing here limits any rights you have under the consumer protection laws that apply to you.
